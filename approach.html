Approach

From ambiguity to something people can actually use.

I like messy problems. The work is figuring out what matters, what connects, and what can become simpler without losing what makes it useful.

01

How I design

  • Start with the people.

    Understand who is experiencing the problem, what they are trying to do, and what is getting in the way.

  • Make something.

    A concrete draft, workflow, prototype, or learning experience gives people something real to react to.

  • Listen. Look at the evidence. Change it.

    Feedback and data are useful when they change the next version, not when they sit in a report.

  • Think beyond launch.

    The experience has to work after handoff: support, maintenance, QA, adoption, and iteration are part of the design.

  • Document the why.

    Make the decisions visible so the next person can understand what changed, what stayed, and why.
