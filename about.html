02

The thread

The work kept getting bigger, but the question stayed familiar: what would make this clearer and easier to use?

I started with individual learning experiences, then moved outward into pathways, support systems, digital products, and program structures. The through-line is still people trying to learn inside complicated systems.

03

Research & standards

How it shows up

My design foundation started with standards-aligned education and has expanded into research-informed learning and product work. Across teaching, graduate research, assessment preparation, WGU program work, and independent product design, I use validated source material, external frameworks, user evidence, and research to inform decisions rather than designing from assumptions.

04

Personal philosophy

I care a lot about making complicated things easier to use without stripping away what makes them useful in the first place. Most of my favorite projects started messy: too much information, too many people involved, or no obvious answer. That's usually the part I enjoy figuring out.
