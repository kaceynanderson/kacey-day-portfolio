About

Designing at a larger level each time.

My career has moved from designing individual learning experiences to designing the programs, products, and systems around them. The settings have changed, but the work has kept building on the same foundation: understand what people need, make complex things clearer, and create something that works in the real world.

02

Experience Behind the Work

Western Governors University

Senior Program Mentor, School of Technology

CBE · Program Design · Faculty Enablement · Educational Systems

My work at WGU started closest to the learner and expanded outward. Alongside direct student support, I lead and contribute to work involving new-program launches, faculty readiness, FLIGHT, learning experience design, facilitation, QA, change and adoption, and reusable systems.

That proximity to learners matters to how I design. I see what happens after a program, tool, or process reaches the people expected to use it.

Kacey Anderson LLC

Founder, Learning & Product Designer

Product Design · Learning Experience Design · Independent Practice

My independent work brings together educational design and product thinking. I design digital learning experiences, assessments, workflows, visual systems, and learner-facing products.

Lumavi became the clearest extension of that work: designing a multi-role educational product around learner progress, tutor workflows, family visibility, administrative needs, and the tension between personalization and scalability.

Newport Healthcare

Academic Coordinator

Individualized Programming · Multidisciplinary Collaboration

At Newport, academic programming couldn't exist separately from the rest of the learner's circumstances. I coordinated individualized pathways across school requirements, accommodations, academic progress, treatment schedules, transitions, families, educators, and treatment teams.

That experience shaped the systems perspective I still bring to design: changing one part of an experience usually affects several others.

Teaching + Academic Coaching

Standards · Assessment · Differentiation · Adaptive Learning

Teaching built my foundation in standards-aligned design, assessment, differentiation, and evidence-informed instruction.

Academic and test-preparation coaching extended that into personalized learning pathways, ACT/SAT preparation, adaptive modules, performance analysis, executive-functioning support, and ongoing adjustment based on what learner evidence showed.

03

Research, Education & Credentials

Research & Standards

Research has been part of my work since the beginning.

My foundation includes standards-aligned curriculum design, assessment specifications, graduate research, program source material, and performance evidence. For new-program readiness work, conflicting source details are marked unverified and routed for confirmation before they enter lessons or faculty guidance.

The context changes, but the expectation stays the same: know what you're designing against and don't build from assumptions.

What Matters to Me

I care about making complicated things easier to use without stripping away what makes them useful.

I’m drawn to work with a lot of moving pieces, different perspectives, and no obvious answer. The part I enjoy most is making sense of those pieces and creating something that works better for the people who have to use it.
