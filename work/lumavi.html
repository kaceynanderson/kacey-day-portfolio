01

The problem

Tutoring and coaching work can become fragmented across learner progress, goals, scores, session notes, family communication, planning, and service management. The design problem was not simply “put tutoring online.” It was making those moving pieces easier to understand and act on.

Public beta today

AI study plans · score trajectory · parent transparency · structured session notes · consultation intake · billing summaries

02

Why I built it

The objective was not merely to digitize coaching. The challenge was creating enough structure to make the work repeatable while preserving the personalization that makes coaching useful in the first place.

Core tension

How do I make this repeatable without making the learner experience feel generic?

05

Product architecture

Four user roles sit on top of shared learner information, workflows, service structures, and progress/status infrastructure. The experience changes by role because the decisions change by role.

Shared product layers

User roles · learner information · planning · session workflows · service management · progress/status infrastructure · family visibility

06

Interaction design

Admins need visibility across students, sessions, and scores. Tutors need the information required before, during, and after sessions. Students need progress, goals, practice, and a clear next step. Parents need curated progress visibility and family updates.

The information boundary is part of the interaction design: not every role should see every field simply because the system stores it.

Role principle

Show the information that supports the decision this person has to make next.

07

Visual system

The interface uses consistent hierarchy, dark surfaces, gradient accents, status treatments, and clear next-action patterns so dense information stays scannable.

09

Real-world use

Lumavi is currently an invite-only private-beta platform for tutoring companies. SarmiereEC is identified publicly as an early partner, while broader testimonials are still being gathered.

Evidence available today

The product is live in private beta with real role-based workflows and an early partner. I am not claiming broad adoption, usage metrics, or product outcomes that are not yet established.

10

What I learned

Personalization ↔ Scalability

Building an actual product made the trade-offs much less theoretical. Reusable structure can create clarity and reduce repeated work, but too much structure can flatten the learner experience. The design work is deciding what deserves a standard and what still needs room for judgment.
